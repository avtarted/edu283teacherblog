Values, Expectatiions, Routines, and Rituals

Classroom Values:

  1. Critical Thinking: My goal is for all my students to develop and exercise critical thinking. Memorizing theorems and procedures is insufficient. I will hold students to rigorous standards, and we will work on proofs and justifications.
  2. Collaboration: When your child enters the workforce, they will need to collaborate with their coworkers. Through group projects, students will practice dividing up tasks, different modes of communication, and consensus-building.

Expectations for me:

  1. Quality Instruction: I will present the course material in a clear and understandable manner, modeling critical thinking and problem solving strategies.
  2. Availability and Responsiveness: Students may schedule lunchtime appointments with me for extra assistance, and I will reply to student and parent/guardian emails within two business days.
  3. Prompt grading: I will try to grade all assignments within three business days.

Expectations for students:

  1. Academic Integrity: Students must not use any assistance on exams. It is ok to interrogate AI for explanations on your take-home assignments, but I expect students to first attempt questions by themselves before turning to AI for help.
  2. Golden Rule: Treat your fellow students and me the way you would like to be treated.
  3. Food: Snacks are permitted provided everyone cleans up after themselves.
  4. Attention: Students must pay attention and stay on-task. Using electronic devices is not permitted unless explicitly allowed.
  5. Punctuality: All assignments must be completed on time.

Routines:

  1. Warm-up: We will discuss prior learnings and work through an example problem.
  2. Agenda: I will review the day’s agenda.
  3. Exit Ticket: A short assessment to evaluate student understanding.

Rituals:

  1. Differential Calculus Signature Project: This is a group project at the midway point between the completion of Differential Calculus and the start of Integral Calculus. Groups will complete a project of their choice connecting to real-life applications of Differential Calculus.
  2. Post-AP Exam Party!

Instructional Strategies

  • Desmos: This is an online graphing calculator that's the standard for state testing: SAT/ACT and AP exams. It's free (no need for students to buy a graphic calculator) and it's more powerful. Desmos has sliders you can toggle to change values and see the changes reflect live on the graph, and I can use it for various hands-on exploration tasks.
  • Worked out fake proofs/problems: Show students an incorrectly worked out example and have them identify the error step.
  • Know-Wonder-Learned(KWL) Chart: This is a chart with three columns to fill in what students know, what they wonder about or want to learn, and finally what they have learned.
  • Manipulatives: Tangible objects that students can physically manipulate to illustrate mathematical principles.
  • Relevant, real-life Math applications: Give students problems that connect to their daily lives, interests, and ambitions to increase their motivation in mastering the material.
